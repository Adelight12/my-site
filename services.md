Apple Roofing provides residential and commercial roofing across Toronto and the GTA, from planned projects to urgent repairs.

## Residential Roofing

- **Shingle Roofing:** professional shingle roof installation, repair and replacement.
- **Concrete Shingle Roofs:** strong, weather-resistant concrete shingle systems.
- **Residential Flat Roofs:** reliable flat roofing solutions for homes.
- **Slate Shingle Roofing:** premium slate roofing built for beauty and durability.
- **Roof Repair & Maintenance:** repairs and upkeep to extend your roof's lifespan.
- **Roof Inspections:** thorough inspections to detect issues early.
- **Drone Roof Inspections:** fast, safe and accurate assessments from above.
- **Skylight Installation:** professional installation for brighter living spaces.
- **Eavestrough Cleaning:** clear gutters for proper drainage and roof protection.
- **Snow Removal:** safe roof snow removal to prevent winter damage.

## Commercial Roofing

- **Commercial Flat Roofs:** durable flat roofing installation and repair for businesses.
- **Commercial Roof Repair & Maintenance:** keep your commercial roof performing at its best.
- **Commercial Roof Inspections:** professional inspections to identify problems early.

## Urgent Roofing Services

- **Emergency Roof Repair:** fast response for urgent roofing problems.
- **Roof Leak Repair:** quick, reliable leak detection and repair.
- **Storm Damage Roof Repair:** repairs for wind, rain, hail and storm damage.
- **Ice Dam Removal:** safe removal to reduce trapped water, leaks and roof-edge damage.
